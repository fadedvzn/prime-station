Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Prime Station ("Prime Station," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the Prime Station website and services.

Important: Prime Station is a developing service. The features and integrations available to you may change as the service is built and reviewed.

Information you provide

We may process information you submit to use the service, such as account or profile details, prompts, messages, settings, and content or media you choose to upload or manage.

Connected platform accounts

If you choose to connect a third-party platform, Prime Station may receive account identifiers, profile information, and authorization tokens or permissions needed to provide the integration you request. The information available depends on the platform and permissions you approve. You can revoke access through the third-party platform's account settings.

How information is used

Information may be used to operate, secure, maintain, and improve Prime Station; provide requested integrations and publishing workflows; respond to support requests; and prevent abuse or technical issues.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that help host or operate Prime Station, or disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect the service and its users. When you direct Prime Station to send content to a connected platform, that platform receives the information required to carry out your request and handles it under its own policies.

Storage and security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Access tokens and other sensitive credentials should be protected and are not intended to be shared publicly.

Retention and your choices

Information is retained only as needed for the service's operation, security, legal obligations, or the purposes described above. You may disconnect third-party accounts through the relevant platform and may contact the service operator to ask about access, correction, or deletion of information associated with your use.

Children

Prime Station is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy as Prime Station changes. The updated date above indicates when this page was most recently revised.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the support contact provided by the Prime Station operator.
