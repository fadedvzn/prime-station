# Kaventra AI — Client Acquisition & Marketing Playbook

## Initial target market
Start with one segment for the first 30 days: local service businesses (auto detailing/car washes, home services, gyms, salons, and independent professional services). Use discovery interviews to choose the segment with the clearest recurring need and willingness to pay.

## Core offers (pilot pricing hypotheses)
- **Growth Marketing:** social calendar, content, profile optimization and monthly reporting — $500–$1,200/month.
- **Content Studio:** short-form video concepts/scripts, editing and creative package — $300–$1,000/month.
- **Website & Conversion:** landing page or small business website with lead form — $750–$2,500/project.
- **AI Workflow Setup:** map, configure and test one lead capture or admin workflow — $750–$2,000 setup.
- **Full-Service Growth:** combined marketing, creative, web and automation — scope-based monthly retainer.

Prices are test assumptions, not market guarantees. Quote based on scope, revisions, ad spend, software costs and delivery hours.

## Prospect process
1. Define niche, service area, business size and qualification criteria.
2. Research public business websites and official business contact channels.
3. Save source URL, date checked, fit score, and a truthful personalization note.
4. Deduplicate and respect do-not-contact requests.
5. Prepare a short audit or useful growth idea, not a generic mass pitch.
6. Draft personalized outreach and proposal for owner review.
7. Owner approves exact recipient, channel, copy and timing before any send.
8. Track replies, calls, proposals, wins/losses, next steps and opt-outs.

## Marketing engine for Kaventra
- Publish educational case-style content, clearly labeling demos and internal experiments.
- Build a website with separate service pages and a clear discovery-call CTA.
- Use short-form video to explain practical business problems and solutions.
- Build SEO around specific service + industry + location searches only where genuinely served.
- Run paid advertising only after landing page, tracking, offer economics and owner-approved budget are ready.
- Send email campaigns only to appropriately sourced contacts with required identification and opt-out mechanisms.

## Example outreach draft
Subject: A growth idea for [Business Name]

Hi [First Name],

I came across [Business Name] while researching [industry]. I noticed [specific, verifiable observation]. Kaventra AI helps businesses improve their marketing and customer workflows. I have a couple of practical ideas that may help [specific business outcome].

Would you be open to a short conversation next week? If this isn't relevant, no worries.

[Owner Name]
Kaventra AI

Draft only. Verify all personalization and approve the exact recipient and message before sending.

## Weekly scorecard
Track qualified prospects, approved/sent outreach, positive replies, calls booked, proposals, clients won, revenue collected, delivery hours, gross margin, campaign performance and client retention.

## Guardrails
No bulk spam, fabricated personalization, fake case studies, guaranteed leads or revenue claims, or unauthorized use of client data. Follow applicable email, phone/SMS, privacy, advertising and platform rules. Honor opt-outs. No advertising spend or external commitments without owner approval.
