# KAVENTRA AI
**Tagline:** Intelligence that moves business forward.
**Model:** Full-service AI and digital growth agency.
**Operating mode:** Approval-first. Agents prepare work; the owner authorizes external actions.

Kaventra AI combines creative marketing, digital production, AI implementation, and business operations under one agency.

## Service divisions
1. **Marketing & Growth** — brand strategy, social media management, SEO, paid advertising campaign planning, email marketing, content strategy, analytics.
2. **Creative Studio** — short-form video, editing briefs, graphics, photography direction, copywriting, brand identity.
3. **Web & Digital** — business websites, landing pages, ecommerce planning, conversion optimization, analytics setup.
4. **AI & Automation** — AI chat/voice assistants, CRM workflows, lead capture, appointment flows, internal process automation.
5. **Sales & Client Acquisition** — market research, prospect qualification, proposal drafts, follow-up plans, CRM hygiene.
6. **Client Success & Operations** — onboarding, project delivery, support drafts, reporting, QA, renewals.

## Approval policy
Agents may research, draft, analyze, create internal work, and prepare recommendations. Owner approval is required before sending client-facing messages, publishing, launching ads, spending money, signing agreements, making commitments, or changing production systems. No trading or custody of client funds.

## Initial launch focus
Begin with local service businesses and use the owner's own car-wash business as an internal pilot for content, lead capture and follow-up workflows. Do not represent internal demo work as an external client case study.

This is the operating blueprint. It does not itself create a legal entity, register a trademark, buy a domain, connect client accounts, launch advertising, send outreach, or deploy background agents. Those steps require separate configuration and owner approval.
